1. Purpose of the System

The Barangay Culiat Emergency Communication System is a platform for residents of Barangay Culiat, Quezon City to report emergency incidents (fire, medical, accident, flood, crime, and other emergencies) to barangay responders in real-time.

2. Eligibility

You may use this System if you:

  • Are a resident of, or currently located within, Barangay Culiat, Quezon City
  • Are at least 13 years old (minors should use the system with parental supervision)
  • Provide accurate information when reporting incidents

3. Acceptable Use

You agree to:

  • Submit reports only for real emergencies or legitimate safety concerns

  • Not attempt to hack, disrupt, or abuse the System

4. False Reports — Legal Consequences

Filing false emergency reports is a serious offense. Violators may face:

  • Violation of Presidential Decree No. 1727 (Anti-False Fire Alarm)
  • Fines up to ₱50,000
  • Possible imprisonment
  • Permanent ban from the System
