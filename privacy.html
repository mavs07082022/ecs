Privacy Policy

Barangay Culiat Emergency Communication System · Last updated: September 2026

The Barangay Culiat Emergency Communication System ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of all residents of Barangay Culiat, Quezon City. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our emergency reporting system, including but not limited to our website, Facebook Messenger bot, and mobile interfaces.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit an emergency report (name, contact number, location, incident description, photos/videos)
  • Register as a resident user of the system
  • Message our Facebook Page for emergency reporting
  • Contact us through other channels

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Dispatching emergency responders to your location
  • Verifying and coordinating emergency reports
  • Communication between you, responders, and barangay officials
  • Record-keeping for incident documentation and analysis
  • Improving our emergency response services

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with:

  • Verified barangay responders handling your specific incident
  • Barangay officials with administrative access
  • Emergency services (PNP, BFP, Medical) when required

4. Data Security

Your data is protected with enterprise-grade encryption, secure authentication, and role-based access controls. Only authorized personnel can access your information.

5. Data Retention

Incident records are retained for documentation and analysis purposes. You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission
